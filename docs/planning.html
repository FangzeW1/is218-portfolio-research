Design idea: chat-first applications

Professor's guidance · An approach to consider across projects, not a separate project.

Core idea: Design the application around one excellent mobile-friendly conversation instead of many pages.

  • Mobile first: Prioritize touch controls, readable messages, and small-screen layout.
  • Conversation first: Let users type, speak, or send images and other media when the workflow needs them.
  • Inline interactions: Put forms, confirmations, and results inside the chat when text alone is insufficient.
  • Accessibility: Support keyboard and screen-reader use, clear labels, focus order, and non-voice alternatives.
  • Privacy: Limit data collection, protect uploaded media and chat history, and explain retention and permissions.

Design test: Can someone complete the core task on a phone without navigating through multiple screens?

Tradeoffs to revisit

Chat is useful for flexible requests, but structured controls may be faster for precise input, browsing large results, or repeated tasks. Keep accessibility and user control ahead of a strict “chat only” rule.
